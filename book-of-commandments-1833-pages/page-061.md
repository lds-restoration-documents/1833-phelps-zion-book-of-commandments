1 *A Revelation to Emma, given in Harmony, Pennsylvania, July,* 1830. <!-- uncertain: a blot follows "Harmony," at the line end -->

<!-- the next paragraph opens with a large drop-capital E and no verse number -->EMMA, my daughter in Zion, a revelation I give unto you, concerning my will:

2 Behold thy sins are forgiven thee, and thou art <!-- uncertain: the "t" of "art" is blotted --> an elect lady, whom I have called.

3 Murmur <!-- uncertain: "Murmur" is overprinted at the end; the "r" runs into the space before "not" --> not because of the things which thou hast not seen, for they are withheld from thee, and from the world, which is wisdom in me in a time to come.

5 And thou shalt go with him at the time of his going, and be unto him for a scribe, that I may send Oliver whithersoever I will.

6 And thou shalt be ordained under his hand to expound scriptures, and to exhort the church, according as it shall be given thee by my Spirit:

10 And verily I say unto thee, that thou shalt lay aside the things of this world, and seek for the things of a better.
